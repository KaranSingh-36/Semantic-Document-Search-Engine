Artificial intelligence is the field of computer science concerned with building systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence. These tasks include understanding language, recognising images, planning, and making decisions under uncertainty. Early AI research focused on symbolic reasoning and hand-written rules, while modern AI relies heavily on learning patterns from data. Applications range from virtual assistants and recommendation systems to medical image analysis and autonomous vehicles. Researchers also study the ethical questions raised by AI, such as fairness, transparency and accountability.
